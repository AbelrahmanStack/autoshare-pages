Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13-09-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoShare collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use the AutoShare mobile application and related services (the "Platform"). AutoShare is a carpooling platform that connects members who wish to share a car journey and split the travel costs.

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is:

AutoShare
Email: info@autoshareit.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account & profile: full name, email address, mobile phone number, password, date of birth, gender, profile photo, mini-bio, preferred language and travel preferences (e.g. chat, music, smoking, pets).
  • Identity & verification: government-issued ID, selfie/photo, phone number (via OTP), email verification, and any documents you upload to become a verified member or driver.
  • Vehicle information (drivers): make, model, colour, year, registration/plate, seats offered and vehicle photos or documents.
  • Ride & booking data: departure and arrival points, stopovers, route, date and time, number of seats, price/contribution per seat, booking requests and history.
  • Payments & payouts: transaction amounts and history, wallet balance, and payout details such as bank account, card, PayPal or mobile-money information you enter for withdrawals. Full card numbers are handled by our payment processors and are not stored on our servers.
  • Communications: in-app chat messages between members, ratings and reviews, reports, and messages you send to customer support.
  • Referrals: information related to referral codes you share or redeem.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Location data: with your permission, precise (GPS) and/or approximate device location, used to show nearby rides, set pickup/drop-off points, autocomplete addresses and improve matching. You can disable location access in your device settings; some features will not work without it.
  • Device & usage data: device model, operating system, app version, language, unique device identifiers, IP address, crash logs, diagnostics and information about how you interact with the Platform.
  • Push notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token used to deliver notifications about bookings, messages and account activity.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used within in-app web views (for example during payment or map flows) to enable functionality and security.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Social / platform sign-in: if you register with Google or Apple, we receive basic profile information (such as name and email) permitted by those services.
  • Payment processors: confirmation of payment status, fraud signals and limited transaction details.
  • Mapping and geolocation providers: address, place and route information based on the locations you enter.
  • Other members: ratings, reviews and reports submitted about you.

3. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Enable you to publish rides, search rides, request and confirm bookings, and match passengers with drivers.
  • Process payments, hold funds in your in-app wallet, calculate service fees and taxes, and process withdrawals/payouts.
  • Verify identity, vehicles and documents, and promote safety, trust and fraud prevention.
  • Enable communication between members (chat, booking updates) and send transactional notifications.
  • Provide customer support and handle disputes, reports and claims.
  • Operate referral and rating features.
  • Monitor, analyse and improve the Platform, and develop new features.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • Send service-related and, where permitted, promotional communications. You can opt out of marketing at any time.

4. Legal bases for processing (where applicable)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract – to provide the Platform and the services you request.
  • Consent – for location access, push notifications, marketing and any optional features. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests – for security, fraud prevention, service improvement and protecting our rights, balanced against your interests.
  • Legal obligation – for accounting, tax, and responding to lawful requests.

5. How we share your information

  • With other members: when you publish or book a ride, we share information needed to complete the trip, such as your first name, profile photo, ratings, member since date, verification badges, bio, travel preferences, vehicle details (for drivers), approximate or agreed pickup point and, once a booking is confirmed, contact by in-app chat and phone number where enabled.
  • Payment and payout providers: such as Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, Paystack, PayFast, Flutterwave, Mercado Pago, Midtrans, Xendit and similar providers, to process transactions and withdrawals.
  • Infrastructure and cloud services: Google Firebase and Google Cloud (authentication, database, storage, hosting, messaging, remote configuration, app check) and related sub-processors.
  • Maps and location services: Google Maps Platform and geocoding providers.
  • Analytics, crash reporting and communications tools that help us operate and improve the Platform.
  • Professional advisers, auditors and insurers where necessary.
  • Authorities and third parties when required by law, court order or to protect the rights, safety and property of members, the public or AutoShare.
  • Corporate transactions: such as a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. International transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect your information when it is transferred internationally.

7. Data retention

We keep your personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform. After account closure, we retain certain data as required for legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, safety and fraud-prevention purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymised. Chat messages, ride history and transaction records are retained for the periods required by applicable law.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls and secure infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your login credentials confidential and notify us immediately of any unauthorised use.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Request portability of information you provided to us.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@autoshareit.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data from within the app (Profile & settings) or by emailing info@autoshareit.com. Some information may be retained as described in Section 7. Outstanding bookings, disputes or wallet balances may need to be resolved before deletion is completed.

11. Children

The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Third-party links and services

The Platform may link to or integrate third-party websites and services (for example payment pages and maps). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for them.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date and, where changes are significant, provide additional notice in the app. Your continued use of the Platform after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
